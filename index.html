隐私声明

本声明说明 Cuteseason Ads Tool 在提供亚马逊广告管理和报表分析服务时，如何处理账户授权信息及广告数据。

运营方：Cuteseason

联系邮箱：del_sd@brightstarx.cn

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 16 日

1. 适用范围与授权

本工具用于管理 Cuteseason 自身及客户授权的亚马逊广告账户。仅有权代表账户授权的用户可以连接账户。针对客户账户，具体服务内容和操作范围以客户授权及适用的服务约定为准。

2. 处理的信息

根据启用的功能及授予的权限，工具可能访问或处理以下信息：

  • 广告账户标识、账户名称、站点及币种等账户信息；
  • 广告活动、广告组、广告、投放目标、关键词、竞价及预算等设置；
  • 展示、点击、花费、销售归因及其他广告效果报表数据；
  • 访问 Amazon Ads API 所需的授权令牌，以及排查连接或服务问题所需的技术记录；
  • 客户在联系或申请支持时主动提供的联系方式和问题描述。

亚马逊登录过程由亚马逊处理。本工具不要求客户向 Cuteseason 提供亚马逊账户密码。

3. 信息的用途

相关信息用于连接已授权的账户、读取和分析广告报表、提供广告管理建议、执行授权用户要求的广告管理操作，以及维护服务和处理客户支持请求。客户账户数据仅用于约定的服务目的。

4. 数据保存与服务提供方

为提供广告分析和管理服务，Cuteseason 会使用自身工作环境及第三方工具处理客户授权的广告数据。第三方工具可能包括 OpenAI 提供的 ChatGPT、Codex，以及在相应服务安排中向客户说明的其他服务。

提交给第三方的内容可能包括与分析任务相关的广告报表、广告设置、账户或站点标识，以及用户提供的任务说明。Cuteseason 将数据使用限制在约定服务所需范围内，并在使用相关第三方处理客户数据前，向客户说明用途及处理方，取得必要授权。

第三方服务中的数据保存、使用和处理地点取决于实际使用的产品、账户设置及适用条款，可能涉及境外处理。客户可以通过联系邮箱了解其账户适用的工具及处理安排，或提出不使用特定第三方工具的要求。此类要求可能影响可提供的服务范围。

服务所需的授权令牌用于建立和维持 API 连接，不属于应公开披露的信息。Cuteseason 不要求客户在普通聊天内容中提交亚马逊密码或应用密钥。

5. 保留期限与删除

广告数据及授权信息仅在提供约定服务、维护服务或履行适用义务所需期间保留。客户可通过上述邮箱申请删除。处理请求时可能需要核实申请人与相关账户的关系；如存在必须继续保留的数据，应向申请人说明原因及适用的保留安排。

6. 撤回授权

客户可以通过亚马逊提供的应用授权管理入口撤回本工具的访问权限，也可联系 Cuteseason 请求停止服务。撤回授权会停止后续授权访问，但不会自动删除已保存的报表或其他副本；这些数据依照本声明及适用的服务约定处理。

7. 声明网页与更新

本声明网页不设置广告、分析追踪脚本或信息收集表单。网站托管服务可能为交付页面和维护安全处理必要的网络访问记录。若工具的用途或数据处理方式发生变化，Cuteseason 将更新本声明及更新日期；涉及新的数据用途或第三方处理时，应在启用相关处理前向客户说明并取得所需授权。

8. 联系我们

如需了解数据处理方式，或提出访问、更正、删除及撤回授权相关请求，请联系 del_sd@brightstarx.cn。

Privacy Notice

This notice describes how Cuteseason Ads Tool handles account authorization information and advertising data when providing Amazon advertising management and reporting services.

Operator: Cuteseason

Contact: del_sd@brightstarx.cn

Last updated: September 16, 2026

1. Scope and authorization

The tool manages Cuteseason's own Amazon advertising accounts and accounts for clients who authorize access. A user must be entitled to authorize access on behalf of an account. Services and permitted actions for client accounts are defined by the client's authorization and the applicable service arrangement.

2. Information handled

Depending on the features enabled and permissions granted, the tool may access or process:

  • Advertising account identifiers, account names, marketplaces and currencies;
  • Campaigns, ad groups, ads, targets, keywords, bids and budgets;
  • Impressions, clicks, spend, attributed sales and other advertising performance reports;
  • Authorization tokens required for the Amazon Ads API and technical records needed to troubleshoot connectivity or service issues;
  • Contact details and issue descriptions that clients provide when requesting support.

Amazon handles the sign-in process. The tool does not ask clients to provide their Amazon account password to Cuteseason.

3. Use of information

Information is used to connect authorized accounts, retrieve and analyze advertising reports, provide advertising management recommendations, perform actions requested by authorized users, maintain the service and respond to support requests. Client account data is used only for the agreed service purposes.

4. Storage and service providers

To provide advertising analysis and management services, Cuteseason processes authorized client advertising data in its own working environments and through third-party tools. These tools may include ChatGPT and Codex provided by OpenAI, and other services identified to the client in the relevant service arrangement.

Content submitted to these providers may include advertising reports, advertising settings, account or marketplace identifiers and task instructions relevant to the analysis. Cuteseason limits data use to what is needed for agreed services, explains the purposes and providers to clients before the processing, and obtains necessary authorization.

Retention, use and processing locations within third-party services depend on the products, account settings and applicable terms actually used, and may involve processing outside the client's country. Clients may contact Cuteseason to learn which tools and arrangements apply to their account or request that a particular third-party tool not be used. Such requests may affect the available services.

Authorization tokens needed to establish and maintain API connections are not intended for public disclosure. Cuteseason does not ask clients to submit Amazon passwords or application secrets in ordinary chat messages.

5. Retention and deletion

Advertising data and authorization information are retained only as necessary to provide agreed services, maintain the service or meet applicable obligations. Clients may request deletion using the contact email above. Cuteseason may need to verify the requester's relationship to the relevant account. Where continued retention is required, the reason and applicable retention arrangements should be explained to the requester.

6. Withdrawing authorization

Clients can revoke the tool's access through Amazon's application authorization controls or contact Cuteseason to request an end to services. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not automatically erase previously stored reports or other copies. Such data is handled under this notice and the applicable service arrangement.

7. This notice website and updates

This notice page contains no advertising, analytics tracking scripts or data collection forms added by Cuteseason. The hosting provider may process network access records needed to deliver the page and maintain security. Cuteseason will update this notice and its date when relevant purposes or practices change. New data uses or third-party processing should be explained to clients, and any required authorization obtained, before that processing begins.

8. Contact

For questions about data handling, or requests relating to access, correction, deletion or withdrawal of authorization, contact del_sd@brightstarx.cn.
